CodeBridge - starta appen / how to run
======================================

1. Dubbelklicka pa "CodeBridge.app" i denna mapp (eller i mappen Program
   efter att du dragit dit appen fran DMG:en).
2. Om macOS fragar "kan inte verifiera utvecklaren": hogerklicka pa appen,
   valj Oppna, och klicka Oppna igen. Detta galler bara tills appen ar
   signerad och notariserad.
3. Appen startar sin egen lokala tjanst automatiskt. Inget annat behover
   installeras - ingen Node.js, Homebrew eller terminal.
4. Kontrollera att allt ar igang: oppna http://127.0.0.1:4317 i en
   webblasare - da ska CodeBridge-appen visas.

Forsta gangen (i appen):
- Valj vilka projektmappar CodeBridge far komma at.
- Klistra in din ngrok-kod (gratis konto pa ngrok.com) nar appen ber om det.
- Folj guiden for att koppla din Custom GPT i ChatGPT.

---

English:
Double-click CodeBridge.app (or drag it to Applications from the DMG first).
If macOS says the developer cannot be verified, right-click the app, choose
Open, then Open again. The app bundles its own Node runtime and ngrok - no
installations are needed. First time: choose project folders, paste your free
ngrok authtoken, and follow the in-app guide to connect your Custom GPT.
