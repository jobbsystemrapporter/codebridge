CodeBridge - Getting Started, the whole way
===========================================

What is CodeBridge?
CodeBridge is a Mac app that lets ChatGPT work with your code projects from a
regular ChatGPT conversation. ChatGPT is the AI. CodeBridge is the secure
bridge to your Mac. You do not need to install anything - no Node.js, no
Homebrew and no terminal. Everything is bundled in the app.

All you need:
- a Mac running macOS 14 or newer (Intel or Apple Silicon),
- internet access,
- a free ngrok account (for the secure connection),
- a regular ChatGPT subscription.

------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 - Install the app
------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open the DMG file (double-click it).
2. Drag CodeBridge.app into the Applications folder (the shortcut is right
   there in the DMG window).
3. Open CodeBridge from Applications or Launchpad - NOT from the disk image
   window. An app run from the image stops working once it is ejected.

CodeBridge is a standalone app that is not registered with Apple, so macOS
will warn you the first time you open it. This is expected, and it is a
one-time step:

  On macOS 15 and newer (including macOS 26):
    1. Try to open CodeBridge. macOS refuses and shows a warning.
    2. Open System Settings -> Privacy & Security.
    3. Scroll down. Next to "CodeBridge.app was blocked", click "Open Anyway".
    4. Confirm with Touch ID or your password.

  On macOS 14:
    Right-click CodeBridge.app -> Open -> Open.

After this, CodeBridge opens normally every time.

------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 - Choose project folders
------------------------------------------------------------------
1. In the app, click "Add folder" (or "Browse" to pick a folder).
2. Select the project folders CodeBridge is allowed to work with.
3. Click Continue.

Only the folders you choose can be reached by ChatGPT. Sensitive locations
(SSH keys, cloud credentials, Keychain) are blocked even if requested.

------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 - Secure connection (ngrok)
------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Create a free account at ngrok.com if you do not have one.
2. In the app, click "Open ngrok". Sign in and copy your connection code
   (authtoken) from the ngrok page. Paste it into the app.
3. In the ngrok dashboard open "Domains" and create your free reserved domain
   (for example example.ngrok-free.app). Paste it into the app's
   "Your reserved address" field.
   WHY: without it, ngrok gives you a NEW address every restart, and your
   custom GPT stops reaching this Mac until you paste a new schema.
4. Click "Connect securely".

The app now creates a private, secure internet address to your Mac. This is
transport only - it is not the OpenAI API and not an AI model.

------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4 - Connect ChatGPT (Custom GPT)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Follow the in-app guide step by step. In short:

1. Click "Open GPT editor" in the app (chatgpt.com/gpts/editor).
2. Paste the Name, Description and Instructions shown in the app.
3. Under Features: make sure "Code Interpreter & Data Analysis" is ticked.
   (The list also shows Web search and Image generation.)
4. Scroll to Actions and click "Create new action".
5. Once you are on the Action page: go back to CodeBridge and click
   "I opened the Action page". Only now does the app show the Schema step.
6. Copy the Schema from the app and paste it into ChatGPT's Schema field.
7. Next to Authentication, click the GEAR ICON. The field says "None" until
   you do, and the options below only exist inside that box.
8. In the box: set Authentication Type to "API Key", paste the CodeBridge
   secret from the app into the "API Key" field, set Auth Type to "Bearer".
   IMPORTANT: This is NOT an OpenAI API key. It is your own private
   CodeBridge secret. Never share it.
9. Click Save.

------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 5 - Test the connection
------------------------------------------------------------------
1. On the Action page, scroll to "Available actions" and click "Test" on the
   codebridgeHealth row. That is the call that reaches your Mac.
2. Come back to CodeBridge and click "Check connection". You can also save the
   GPT and ask it: "Check CodeBridge connection".
3. The app shows READY - only then is the setup complete.

------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 6 - Start using it
------------------------------------------------------------------
Things you can ask your GPT:
- "Show me my projects"
- "Open this project and explain its structure"
- "Find the bug, fix it and run the tests"
- "Show me what changed in Git"

------------------------------------------------------------------
Security
------------------------------------------------------------------
- CodeBridge can only reach the folders you chose.
- Risky commands require your approval in the app (Allow once / Cancel).
- Your files never leave your Mac unless you ask ChatGPT to work with them.
- The secret in the app is private. Never paste an OpenAI API key instead.

------------------------------------------------------------------
Removing CodeBridge completely
------------------------------------------------------------------
1. In the app, open the "Start over" menu (top right).
2. Choose "Erase all data & start fresh" to wipe everything and begin the
   setup again, or "Uninstall CodeBridge" to remove the app entirely.
3. Confirm the dialog. Uninstall erases all local CodeBridge data
   (configuration, secrets, logs, workspaces), removes the launch agent and
   moves CodeBridge.app to the Trash.
4. Empty the Trash to finish.

Your project folders are never modified or deleted by either option.

------------------------------------------------------------------
Troubleshooting
------------------------------------------------------------------
- The app does not start: open it from Applications, or right-click -> Open.
- No connection: your Mac must be awake and online. Restart the connection in
  the app (step 3).
- The GPT does not answer commands: make sure the Schema and the Bearer secret
  are saved in the Action settings and that "Code Interpreter & Data Analysis"
  is enabled.
- Check that the service is running: open http://127.0.0.1:4317 in a browser -
  the CodeBridge app will be shown.

No Node.js, Homebrew, Xcode or terminal is needed. Everything is bundled.
